Andover Audio SpinBase 2: Specs, Price and Who This Turntable Speaker Is For

Disclosure: this page contains affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change what we write.
Short answer

The Andover Audio SpinBase 2 is an all-in-one powered speaker you set a turntable on top of. It is listed at $349 with 70W of power, a built-in phono preamp, two-way Bluetooth, line-in, optical input, USB playback, a subwoofer output and a remote. Andover offers a 60-day in-home trial and extendable warranties, so you can try it before committing.

Turntable speaker bases are popular with people who want vinyl in a small space without buying and wiring separate components. This guide covers the SpinBase 2 using Andover's own product page and an independent review of the original SpinBase. We have not tested it ourselves.

Andover also lists its IsoGroove vibration-isolation design, which is meant to reduce feedback from the speakers reaching the turntable. The box includes a power adapter, an RCA cable with ground, a remote and a quick start guide.

How is it different from the original SpinBase?

Andover describes the SpinBase 2 as improved, with enhanced sound, a sleeker design and easier operation, but does not list a full spec-by-spec difference. The original SpinBase, reviewed by Sound & Vision in 2020 at $299, offered 18 watts per channel, two stereo RCA inputs and Bluetooth, and the reviewer noted it had no remote control and no input selector. Compared with that review, the SpinBase 2 lists a remote, an optical input and USB playback.

Who is the SpinBase 2 for?

It suits people who want simple vinyl playback with decent sound in a dorm, apartment, office or small room, and who like the idea of one box plus a turntable. The reviewer of the original praised it for easy setup and good performance for its size and described it as a stepping stone to larger systems.

It is less suited to people who want to choose their own amplifier and speakers, or who want a hi-fi system built around separates. If that is you, a turntable speaker base will feel limiting.

What to check before you buy

  • Your turntable: Andover says the SpinBase 2 works with any turntable. If yours has a built-in preamp, check its output setting so you do not amplify the signal twice.
  • Space and placement: the turntable sits on top, so check that your turntable fits the top of the unit and that the unit fits your shelf.
  • Bass: a subwoofer output is available if you want more low end later.
  • Colour and stock: when we checked, black units were shipping and white was on pre-order for a later delivery.
  • Return terms: the 60-day in-home trial is a useful way to confirm it works in your room.

FAQ

Does the SpinBase 2 have a built-in phono preamp?

Yes. Andover lists a built-in phono preamp, so you can connect a turntable that outputs a phono signal directly.

Does the SpinBase 2 have Bluetooth?

Yes. It supports two-way Bluetooth, so it can receive audio from your phone and transmit to Bluetooth headphones. Andover does not list the Bluetooth version on the page we checked.

Can I use any turntable with the SpinBase 2?

Andover says yes. Check your turntable's output type and whether it has a built-in preamp.

How much is the SpinBase 2?

Andover lists it at $349. Prices change, so check the product page.

Does Andover offer a trial?

Andover lists a 60-day in-home trial, free shipping on orders of $100 or more, and extendable warranties.
