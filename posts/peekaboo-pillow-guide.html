What is the Peekaboo Pillow?

It is a wedge-shaped pillow for use on your lap while you are seated. The brand says it holds your baby at an incline so you can see and interact with them during feeding, burping, tummy time and bonding. It has two pacifier loops and two side pockets, and the brand also says elevation can help with reflux, which is a medical question to raise with your pediatrician.

Materials and care

The brand lists a cotton filling and cotton cover, describes the materials as non-toxic, and says the pillow is made in the USA. Care instructions say to spot clean it rather than machine wash it, which is why a spare cover can be useful.

Is the Peekaboo Pillow safe?

Use it only awake and supervised, on your lap, following the brand's guidelines. It is not a place for a baby to sleep or nap. The AAP recommends that babies sleep on their backs on a firm, flat surface such as a crib, bassinet or play yard that meets Consumer Product Safety Commission standards, with nothing but a fitted sheet. It advises keeping pillows, quilts, blankets and soft toys out of the sleep area, and says products not designed for infant sleep, such as nursing pillows, should be avoided for sleep. If your baby falls asleep on any lap pillow, move them to a firm, flat sleep surface on their back.

What to look for in a baby lap pillow

  • Clear instructions that say it is for awake, supervised lap use only.
  • A removable cover that is easy to clean.
  • Materials and manufacturing details you are comfortable with.
  • A realistic delivery date and a return policy you have read.
  • Advice from your pediatrician if your baby has reflux or feeding difficulties.
